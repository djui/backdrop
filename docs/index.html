Backdrop

Your desktop, quietly in motion.

A small menu bar app for macOS that loops any video as your wallpaper, and pauses itself when you’re on battery.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

v1.1.0 · Universal · macOS 16 Tahoe or later · Free

01 — Loop

Point it at a folder. Pick a clip. That’s the setup.

Backdrop scans a folder of mp4, mov and m4v files. Choose one in Settings and it repeats behind your windows until you pick another. Try it here.

03 — Rest

Knows when to sit still.

Unplug, or switch on Low Power Mode, and Backdrop freezes on the current frame. Your desktop still looks good and the battery barely notices. Plug back in and it picks up where it stopped.

Paused · showing last frame

Every display

One desktop-level player per screen, so external monitors loop too.

Stays out of the way

Lives in the menu bar with no Dock icon. Play/Pause, Settings, Quit.

Open at Login

Turn it on once in Settings and your wallpaper is moving when you sign in.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Grab Backdrop-1.1.0.zip and move Backdrop.app to Applications.

  2. 2

    Clear the quarantine flag

    The build isn’t notarized yet, so macOS needs a nudge the first time:

    xattr -cr /Applications/Backdrop.app
  3. 3

    Pick a video

    Settings opens on first launch. Choose a folder and a clip, and you’re done.
